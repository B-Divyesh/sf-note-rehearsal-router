The product

Note Rehearsal Router is a browser extension that reads a user-chosen local Markdown folder and writes separate rehearsal ticket files. The free tier routes up to 30 notes. The lifetime unlock removes that limit and enables custom ticket-folder names.

Purchase and license

The unlock costs US $19 as a one-time purchase unless the hosted checkout states otherwise. One buyer may use the license on devices they control. Do not resell or publish license tokens. Sociobot / Dodo is the merchant of record and handles checkout and refunds. A refund revokes the associated license automatically.

Your files and responsibility

You retain ownership of notes and tickets. The extension is designed not to modify source notes, but software and storage can fail. Keep ordinary backups and review generated tickets before relying on them.
