01 / The problem

Saved isn’t learned.

Folders and backlinks are good at keeping ideas. They are less good at making you retrieve, apply, explain, or dismiss one. Router inserts the missing decision between capture and rehearsal.

“I have a graveyard of good intentions.”— the note-taking problem, in one line

02 / The method

A deliberate five-way junction.

No model chooses for you. The act of choosing is part of learning.

  1. 1

    Recall

    Retrieve the central idea without looking, then check the gaps.

  2. 2

    Solve

    Use the note on one concrete problem and show each step.

  3. 3

    Teach

    Explain it to a beginner with an example and a limitation.

  4. 4

    Do

    Take the smallest real-world step before reopening the note.

  5. 5

    Archive

    Name why it no longer deserves rehearsal, then release it.

03 / Your local flow
